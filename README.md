# Anatomy Pets Clinic — Multi-Page Website

A modern, responsive, high-converting veterinary clinic website built with **React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide Icons** for **Anatomy Pets Clinic**, located in Faisal Town, Lahore, Pakistan.

---

## 📑 Separate Dedicated Pages

The website is structured into distinct, dedicated pages with their own independent URLs and files:

1. **Home (`/` or `index.html` / `Website-Standalone.html`):**
   - Hero with compassionate pet care messaging & dual CTAs
   - Verified 4 core trust highlights
   - Welcome intro with "Learn More" leading to About page
   - Top 3 Featured Services spotlight with "View All 6 Services" button
   - Why Choose Us clinical pillars
   - Doctor spotlight with "View Doctor Profiles & Credentials" button
   - Advance Appointment Scheduling banner
   - Google Reviews snippet
   - Collapsible FAQs

2. **About Us (`/about` or `about.html`):**
   - In-depth clinic story, surgical asepsis standards, fear-reduced animal handling, and clinical expectations.

3. **Veterinary Services (`/services` or `services.html`):**
   - Comprehensive directory of all 6 verified clinical services & surgeries
   - Detailed procedure features, clinical tips, and direct WhatsApp inquiry triggers.

4. **Our Veterinarians (`/doctors` or `doctors.html`):**
   - Dedicated profiles of Dr. Muhammad (Chief Surgeon) and Dr. Ayesha (Feline & Medicine Specialist) with PVMC credentials and consultation booking.

5. **Clinic Gallery (`/gallery` or `gallery.html`):**
   - Filterable photo gallery (Facilities, Care & Surgeries, Patients) with full-screen lightbox modal.

6. **Contact Us (`/contact` or `contact.html`):**
   - Full interactive appointment booking form, helpline (`+92 300 5488211`), WhatsApp chat, hours schedule, and Google Maps directions.

7. **Privacy Policy (`/privacy` or `privacy.html`):**
   - Patient medical confidentiality and owner contact data protection terms.
